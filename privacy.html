Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

PromptWise ("the extension") is developed by YOUR_NAME ("we"). This policy explains what the extension does with your data. In short: we do not collect it.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal information, browsing history, page content or analytics. The extension has no account system, no backend server and no tracking or advertising code.

2. What is stored on your device

To work, the extension saves the following in your browser using the chrome.storage.local API: your prompts, folders, favorites, usage counts, and your Pro license key if you activate one. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it. It is removed if you uninstall the extension or clear its data. If your browser sync or backup is on, your browser vendor may handle that data under its own policy.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • storage: saves your prompts, folders and settings locally.
  • activeTab and scripting: used only when you press Insert. The extension then places your chosen prompt text into the text box of the tab you are viewing. It does not read the page, and it does nothing in the background.

4. Clipboard

When you press Copy, the extension writes your prompt to your clipboard. It never reads your clipboard.

5. Payments and license keys

Pro is sold through a third-party payment provider on its own website. That provider handles your payment details and email under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your card details. Your license key is checked inside the extension and is not sent anywhere.

6. Third parties and remote code

The extension does not load remote code and does not send data to third parties. When you press the Pro purchase button, your browser opens the store page in a new tab.

7. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of any information handled by the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Children

The extension is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.
